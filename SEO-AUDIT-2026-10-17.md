# Search visibility investigation — 17 October 2026

## Evidence

Source: user-supplied Search Console exports dated 17 October. The second workbook's Chart sheet covers 23 September–14 October, despite its “Last 28 days” filter. It does not contain daily data for 15–17 October.

| Date | Clicks | Impressions | Position |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |

| October 11 | 63 | 1,381 | 9.3 |
| October 12 | 34 | 871 | 9.3 |
| October 13 | 14 | 125 | 20.2 |
| October 14 | 14 | 134 | 17.3 |

Visibility starts declining October 12; October 13 has the sharp ranking break. The homepage accounts for 592 of 594 clicks across the export. The separate 24-hour comparison shows declines across mobile and desktop and multiple countries, with several identical queries losing average position. This supports a broad homepage visibility decline, not a demonstrated transfer to another internal URL.

The export does not prove a manual action, deindexing, keyword cannibalization, or a specific algorithmic cause. Google’s ranking dashboard lists a September 24 spam update lasting approximately until October 8; proximity is not causation. Advertising removal on October 14 and extractor fixes on October 16 occurred after the decline began.

## Confirmed defects addressed

- Removed homepage typo/search-term blocks and sitewide repeated keyword paragraphs.
- Replaced generic SEO commentary with download troubleshooting and format guidance.
- Corrected MP3 and 4K promises to actual M4A and up-to-1080p support, including metadata.
- Rewrote the keyword-targeting blog article as practical help at its existing URL.
- Reduced promoted homepage links to distinct video, audio, Shorts and thumbnail tasks.
- Removed unsupported site-search structured data and fixed one-minute HowTo duration claims.
- Removed automatic fresh sitemap timestamps for unchanged pages; retained the article's actual modification date.
- Added www host redirect and attached/verified www.yt1s.video in Vercel to address the observed TLS hostname mismatch.
